Shoe Finder / climbing

Find a climbing shoe that fits your foot.

Narrow the list by downturn, asymmetry, stiffness, width, volume, closure, rubber and toe shape, then put up to four shoes side by side. Each shoe links to its product page.

Why this exists. I wanted to choose shoes by how they fit, not by brand, and I didn't know of any site that let me filter the whole market that way. Shops filter by brand and size, and reviews cover a few models at a time. So I collected the specs myself.

Specs come from manufacturer catalogues and retailers. Width, volume and stiffness are relative judgements and fit differs between feet, so try shoes on before buying. Prices are indicative list prices in EUR.
